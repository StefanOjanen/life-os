# Weekly review

Runs once a week in the evening slot and replaces that evening's check-in.

1. `git pull`. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `now.md`, the week's journals and `growth/capacity.md`.
2. Process replies and captures; triage the inbox.
3. Write `reviews/YYYY-Www.md`:
   - **Wins** and **slipped** items from the week.
   - **Capacity:** energy average and 4-week trend, load against the budget, and whether to flag. If flagged, name the one or two things to pause or drop and ask the owner to choose.
   - **Nudge review:** rows nudged three or more times, parked rows older than four weeks, and anything that should be dropped.
   - **Inbox health:** size, `Action` count, top new senders.
   - **Next week's top 3** and at most three project questions.
4. Send `[Life OS] Weekly review · Www` with the review's headline points, the warning-signs question and the nudge review.
5. Commit `weekly: YYYY-Www` and push to `main`.
