# Evening check-in

1. `git pull`. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `routines/delivery.md`, `now.md` and today's journal.
2. **Replies and captures:** process them as in `routines/delivery.md`.
3. **Light triage:** apply `gmail/rules.md` to `in:inbox newer_than:1d`.
4. **Send** `[Life OS] Evening · <Day DD Mon>` with a few prompts that take two minutes to answer from a phone:
   1. **Done today?** List the `now.md` items that might be done; the owner replies with numbers.
   2. **Stuck on anything?**
   3. **Tomorrow's top 3,** proposed from `now.md` and tomorrow's calendar.
   4. **One reflection,** alternating by day of month (professional on odd days, personal on even days).
   5. **Nudge check:** the one to three most urgent open rows, ending with `Reply e.g. "done N4" · "N4 Fri" · "park N4"`.
   6. **Energy:** `Energy today? reply "energy 1-5"`. Do not chase a missing answer.
   Mention anything due tomorrow.
5. Append an "Evening" section to the day's journal.
6. Commit `evening: YYYY-MM-DD` and push to `main`.
