# Capacity

A weekly workload budget and early warning signs, so the system protects the owner from overcommitting.

## Budget

- Active side projects: <max 2>
- Hours per week outside work: <about 5>
- Everything else is maintenance or paused.

## Early warning signs (edit to your own)

1. Sleeping worse
2. Irritable or short with people
3. Dreading things you usually enjoy
4. Keeping score or quietly resenting people
5. Fantasising about escaping
6. Skipping training, blocks or meals
7. Saying yes on the spot, then regretting it

## How routines use this

- **Evening:** one line, `Energy today? reply "energy 1-5"`. Record the answer in the log. Do not chase a missing answer.
- **Weekly review:** energy average and 4-week trend, load against the budget, and one question: `Any warning signs this week? reply "signs 1 3" or "signs none"`.
- **Flag** when the energy average is 2.5 or lower, it fell two weeks in a row, two or more signs show up two weeks running, or the load is over budget. A flag means: name the one or two things to pause or drop and ask the owner to choose. Never add work in a flagged week.
- **New-commitment check:** before adding anything, say what it costs per week and what it replaces.
